Customer ambience: pick the loop

One loop plays on the customer page only while the Sound plate is on. Each file is about 60 s, joined with a crossfade, level-matched to the same loudness. It plays at volume 0.25, as in the app. Press one to hear it looped; press it again, or "No sound", to stop. Use your screen reader at the same time to check that speech stays easy to hear.
